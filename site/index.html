The Köppen map (1986–2010) labels seven central-India parks 'Mediterranean, hot summer', which is unusual for a monsoon region; the weather clusters, built from our own data, are the more reliable grouping.

How the forecasts are made

  • Parks: 50 parks from the official park-wise list (Lok Sabha Q.3633, Aug 2023). 43 have a verified location and are forecast: 18 already generating, 25 under construction (forecast as if built at sanctioned capacity).
  • Actual output: no park publishes hourly metered output, so it is calculated with pvlib from ERA5 reanalysis weather. On a real plant with its own sensor, this method matches measured output with 0.992 correlation.
  • Model inputs: ECMWF weather forecasts issued a day earlier, recent output, sun position, time of day and park details. The model never sees the weather that actually happened.
  • Models: LightGBM, XGBoost, CatBoost and Random Forest (tree ensembles), LSTM and GRU neural networks, trained on an NVIDIA RTX 4060 GPU where supported.
  • Ranges: LightGBM quantile models calibrated with conformal prediction, so an 80% range contains the actual value about 80% of the time.
  • Testing: train April 2024 to June 2025, calibrate July to December 2025, test January to September 2026. No shuffling: the model never learns from the future.

Read the numbers with these limits

  • Errors are relative to simulated output. Real plants also have outages, curtailment and dust events, which roughly doubled the error on the two real plants tested.
  • Sudden cloud swings (about 1% of hours) are mostly missed.
  • Errors roughly double in the monsoon (June to September).
  • Capacity for generating parks is the Oct-2023 commissioned figure; later additions are not published park by park.
  • This page shows saved test-period forecasts, not a live feed.
